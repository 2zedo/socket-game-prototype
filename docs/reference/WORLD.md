# World Reference

## Role

This is the current reference for CONCENT's world, society, and tone. If world direction changes, update this file and then update `docs/GAME_INFO.md` if the high-level summary changes.

## Core Setting

`CONCENT / 전력 부족의 시대` takes place in THE GRID, a constrained near-future urban system where power, access, and information are rationed. People in lower-grid housing live with limited energy, restricted infrastructure, and controlled information flow.

The game is not post-apocalyptic ruin. It is a managed scarcity society: worn systems still function, but access is unequal.

## Power Shortage Era

- Electricity is scarce enough to shape daily choices.
- Devices are not just props; they compete for limited available power.
- Power management should feel like survival, work preparation, and class pressure at once.
- Failure should usually be warning, efficiency loss, risk increase, or degraded performance before instant game-over.

## Social Layer

- Yui lives in a lower-grid residential unit.
- Lower-grid life should feel cramped, practical, and constrained, not filthy or hopeless.
- Yui is poor enough to feel pressure, but skilled enough to maintain a tidy, functional room.
- The world outside the room is attractive and unreachable: sea, horizon, leisure, open air.

## Tone

Use:

- tidy lower-grid housing
- worn industrial materials
- practical future devices
- controlled city light
- small personal longing
- quiet pressure

Avoid:

- luxury cyberpunk penthouse
- dirty slum caricature
- American motel colors
- villain laboratory
- factory floor
- decorative neon clutter

## Economy

Grid Credit is the candidate reward / currency axis.

Current boundary:

- `GridCreditState` exists as a skeleton.
- No production reward, save-load, story flag, result payout, or job completion economy is wired yet.

## Environment Language

The world should communicate scarcity through:

- limited light
- maintained but old devices
- reused panels and metal
- utility-first furniture
- compact appliances
- organized cables
- nearby building / pipe / sign views rather than open skyline

The world should not communicate poverty through random trash piles or chaotic grime. Yui keeps her space organized.
