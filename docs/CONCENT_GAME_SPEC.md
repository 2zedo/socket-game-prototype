# CONCENT Game Spec

## Game Concept

`CONCENT / 전력 부족의 시대` is a 2D narrative survival adventure about living through a power-starved apartment crisis. The player survives by deciding where limited electricity goes and what comfort, information, or safety is worth spending it on.

## Tone

The tone is quiet, dark, grounded, lonely, and tense. The game should feel more like surviving an uncertain night than winning an arcade challenge.

## Core Loop

1. Start a day with limited power.
2. Inspect the apartment and available objects.
3. Choose whether to spend power on survival, information, comfort, or risk.
4. Receive dialogue or event feedback.
5. Track the consequences of power use.
6. End the day and review the result.

## Key Characters

- Yui: the player protagonist and survivor.
- Delivery robot: a support presence and possible clue carrier.
- Management office staff: grounded human pressure, instructions, and information.
- Grid: a mysterious power/network system tied to the larger truth.

## Core Mechanic

Power management is the core mechanic. Every important action should ask whether spending electricity is worth the tradeoff.

## Long-Term Structure

The long-term story structure targets DAY 1~12, but the current implementation target is only DAY 1. Later days should expand consequences, characters, and mystery after the DAY 1 loop is playable.
