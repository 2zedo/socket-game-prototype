# CONCENT Game Spec

## Game Concept

`CONCENT / 전력 부족의 시대` is a 2D narrative survival adventure about living through a power-starved apartment crisis. The player survives by deciding where limited electricity goes and what comfort, information, or safety is worth spending it on.

## Setting

The game takes place in a small, worn apartment during an era of power shortage. The city outside still exists, but it feels distant and unreliable. Inside the room, outlets, cables, old devices, notices, and weak lights define the player's daily survival space.

## Player Fantasy

The player fantasy is not combat or conquest. It is the feeling of staying alive, making careful choices, and slowly understanding a larger system while isolated in a limited room.

## Tone

The tone is quiet, dark, grounded, lonely, and tense. The game should feel more like surviving an uncertain night than winning an arcade challenge.

The visual and narrative tone should stay restrained: muted beige, brown, dark gray, weak warm light, practical UI panels, and a mood of tired persistence rather than spectacle.

## Core Loop

1. Start a day with limited power.
2. Inspect the apartment and available objects.
3. Choose whether to spend power on survival, information, comfort, or risk.
4. Receive dialogue or event feedback.
5. Track the consequences of power use.
6. End the day and review the result.

## Key Characters

- Yui: the player protagonist and survivor.
- Delivery robot: a support presence and possible clue carrier.
- Management office staff: grounded human pressure, instructions, and information.
- Grid: a mysterious power/network system tied to the larger truth.

## Core Mechanic

Power management is the core mechanic. Every important action should ask whether spending electricity is worth the tradeoff.

Power usage should create tradeoffs between:

- survival and comfort
- information and risk
- contact with others and isolation
- short-term relief and later consequences

## Key Room Objects

- Light: safety, visibility, and atmosphere.
- Laptop: information, logs, and early Grid clues.
- Fan: comfort and survival pressure.
- Charger: practical device survival.
- Communication device: outside contact, notices, and future NPC hooks.

## DAY 1 Role

DAY 1 should teach the power loop with a small set of room interactions. It is a tutorial and proof of the core experience, not a full narrative chapter.

## Long-Term Structure

The long-term story structure targets DAY 1~12, but the current implementation target is only DAY 1. Later days should expand consequences, characters, delivery robot interactions, management office pressure, and Grid mystery after the DAY 1 loop is playable.
